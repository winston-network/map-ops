Why some paths are grey/dashed

The MAP-OPS app filters out polygons with no name + no stat data — they'd be clutter. The editor shows ALL 150 so you can edit or fill in missing info for them. Grey/dashed = currently hidden in the app; pink = visible.

If you fill in stats or a name on a grey one, it turns amber (modified) and will become visible in the app after Barry merges your edits.

Color code

Pink = visible in app, unchanged.
Grey dashed = in the data but hidden by the app's filter.
Amber = you've edited boundary or metadata.
Green = polygons you drew from scratch.
